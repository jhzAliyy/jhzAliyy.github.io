## Research interests

LLM agent systems, inference reliability, and formal analysis of learning-based systems. I work on questions where building a working mechanism and explaining why it works are treated as one requirement rather than two: agent composition without a host process, how much reasoning history a model version can lose before it silently stops completing tasks, and whether inference-time expert deactivation matches the weight rule used in training.
